How it all started.

Scroll down to see how we grew alongside Singapore's AI ecosystem.

2024

In 2024, our dream of bringing AI practitioners together started as casual "AI Wednesdays".

We launched our first community gathering in Funan to share ideas and foster connections.

2025

In 2025, we realised that an occasional meetup wasn't enough to sustain the momentum.

We moved Lorong AI to Chinatown at 22X, establishing a dedicated co-working space for builders.

Bridging the gap
2026

In 2026, we expanded to One-North to be at the physical center of the Singapore tech ecosystem.

We bridged cutting-edge startups with deep research and government innovation.
