Accelerating AI Innovation in Singapore

We bridge the gap between theory and execution. Lorong AI serves as the premier AI hub in Singapore, where AI practitioners, government agencies, industry leaders, and research talent connect. We provide a collaborative environment dedicated to building AI for the public good, transforming national AI strategies into real-world applications.

Bridging Communities

Named after the Malay word for "alley" or "lane," Lorong embodies our commitment to discovery and community-led growth. Since launching our pilot at 22 Cross Street in early 2025 and expanding to the one-north innovation district in 2026, we have cultivated a high-density network designed to foster the serendipitous professional connections that define Singapore's most vibrant innovation clusters.

Dynamic Environment for AI Builders

We provide more than just desk space; we build the connective tissue for the ecosystem. Our facilities integrate dedicated AI co-working infrastructure with high-intensity technical programming. By co-locating builders in Singapore’s strategic innovation hubs, we accelerate the journey from foundational research to scalable, impactful AI implementation.

General & Identity

Lorong AI is a national initiative serving as the community engine for Singapore’s National AI Strategy 2.0 (NAIS 2.0), providing the essential framework to mobilize the nation's AI ecosystem. As a community-driven hub, we bridge the gap between government, industry, and research, blurring the lines between these sectors to foster a high-density environment where policy, innovation, and academic research converge to accelerate real-world AI solutions.

While standard co-working spaces focus on amenities, we focus on collaboration. Every member is part of a curated group of AI practitioners, founders, and researchers. We don't just provide desks; we provide a high-density network of peers building real-world AI applications.

Membership & Benefits

We curate our community to ensure a high-value environment. We welcome AI practitioners, startup founders, researchers, and technical talent who are actively building or researching in the AI space.

Members get 24/7 access to our specialized hubs at 22 Cross Street and one-north, priority access to our 200+ annual technical workshops, and direct inclusion in our member directory—connecting you to Singapore's top AI talent.
