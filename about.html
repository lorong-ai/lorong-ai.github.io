Learn more about our community and how things work at Lorong AI.

Lorong AI is a collaborative hub bringing together government, industry, and research to build Singapore's AI ecosystem. We aim to connect practitioners to build AI for the public good.

Our network includes over 300 core members and a community of 16,000+ AI practitioners. Members span across leading tech companies, research institutions, and various government agencies.
