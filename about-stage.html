2024

In 2024, our dream of bringing AI practitioners together started as casual "AI Wednesdays".

We launched our first community gathering in Funan(or mddi?) to share ideas and foster connections.

2025

In 2025, we realised that an occasional meetup wasn't enough to sustain the momentum.

We moved Lorong AI to Chinatown at 22X, establishing a dedicated co-working space for builders.

2026

In 2026, we expanded to One-North to be at the physical center of the Singapore tech ecosystem and Kampung AI.

With our own space, we run over 5 events weekly, catering to people from all backgrounds.

Lorong AI is a collaborative hub bringing together government, industry, and research to build Singapore's AI ecosystem. We aim to connect practitioners to build AI for the public good.

Our network includes over 300 core members and a community of 16,000+ AI practitioners. Members span across leading tech companies, research institutions, and various government agencies.
