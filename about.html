A Community of Communities.

"Lorong" is a Malay word that translates to "alley" or "lane" in English, evoking a sense of connection, exploration, and community.

In a fast-paced and ever-evolving field like AI, the need for a regular exchange of ideas and insights is critical.

Lorong AI provides a dynamic canvas where practitioners connect, share knowledge, and grow through seamless collaboration and impeccable environments.

Learn more about our community and how things work at Lorong AI.

Lorong AI is a collaborative hub bringing together government, industry, and research to build Singapore's AI ecosystem. We aim to connect practitioners to build AI for the public good.

Our network includes over 300 core members and a community of 16,000+ AI practitioners. Members span across leading tech companies, research institutions, and various government agencies.

Our dedicated spaces at 22X and One-North are designed for cross-sector innovation. Membership provides access to world-class infrastructure and allows you to co-locate with other AI professionals.
